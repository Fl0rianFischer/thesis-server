# Watchlist

One entry per holding. The "Watching for" line is what the briefing filters news
against — the more specific it is, the less noise you get back.

---

## ADBE — Adobe

**Watching for:** Digital Media net-new ARR and the Creative Cloud growth rate,
Firefly and AI-feature monetisation actually showing up in revenue, pricing or
packaging changes, and competitive displacement by generative-AI design tools.

## AMZN — Amazon

**Watching for:** AWS growth rate and backlog, capex guidance and what it is
being spent on, retail operating margin, advertising revenue, and anything that
changes the AI-capacity constraint story.

## ASML — ASML

**Watching for:** Net bookings, especially EUV and High-NA, export-control
changes affecting China sales, capex commentary from TSMC, Intel and Samsung
that lands in ASML's order book, and shipment or acceptance timing shifts.

## DDOG — Datadog

**Watching for:** Revenue growth and net revenue retention, contribution from
AI-native customers and whether that cohort's usage is durable, large-customer
adds, and competitive displacement against Splunk, New Relic and Grafana.

## GOOGL — Alphabet

**Watching for:** Search revenue and any evidence AI Overviews are changing
query monetisation, Cloud growth and backlog, capex, antitrust remedies and
appeals, and Gemini model or distribution announcements.

## MPWR — Monolithic Power Systems

**Watching for:** Design wins and socket share in AI power delivery, any change
in position at Nvidia, gross margin, inventory and lead times, and customer
concentration commentary.

## MSFT — Microsoft

**Watching for:** Azure growth rate and the AI contribution within it, capex and
capacity constraints, Copilot seat adoption and pricing, and changes to the
OpenAI relationship.

## NET — Cloudflare

**Watching for:** Revenue growth, additions of $100k+ customers, Workers and
inference adoption, pricing changes, and any major outage or security incident.

## NOW — ServiceNow

**Watching for:** Subscription revenue growth and cRPO, net new ACV, how much of
it management attributes to Now Assist and AI Pro Plus, US federal exposure, and
pricing-model changes.

## SHOP — Shopify

**Watching for:** GMV growth, take rate and merchant-solutions attach, US
consumer spending as it shows up in merchant volume, and competitive moves by
Amazon.

## TSM — TSMC (ADR)

**Watching for:** The monthly revenue release, advanced-node (N3/N2) capacity,
pricing and utilisation, AI and HPC share of revenue, capex guidance, export
controls, and Taiwan geopolitics.

---

## Macro context

Things to always cover in the macro section, regardless of holdings:

- US index futures and 10Y yield direction
- Fed commentary or scheduled speeches
- EUR/USD, if it moved more than half a percent
- Scheduled releases today: CPI, PCE, payrolls, FOMC

## Not interesting

Tell the briefing what to ignore. This is as useful as telling it what to watch.

- Analyst price target changes and rating upgrades/downgrades
- Listicles ("3 stocks to buy now"), and anything with a headline in that shape
- Routine 13F/insider filings without an unusual size
- Options-volume and "unusual activity" stories
